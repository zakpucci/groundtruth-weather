How this works

Truth
Final NWS climate reports for 25 US stations: the official daily high and low.
Groundtruth
A statistical blend of the inputs. Each day it refits bias, weights and spread from verified results only. The recipe itself is frozen and changes only at a quarterly review.
Live vs hindcast
Live forecasts are committed to a public record before the weather happens and are never edited. Hindcasts are reconstructed from archived model runs, walking forward so no forecast sees its own outcome. Treat them as weaker evidence.
Scoring
MAE is the average miss in °F. CRPS also rewards honest uncertainty and only applies to entries that publish a spread. The comparison uses a 10-day block bootstrap, and a lead is only called real when its 95% interval excludes zero.
The fair bar
The published National Blend uses 12-hour temperature windows rather than the climate day, so we also score it after the same rolling station correction we apply to ourselves. The verdict always uses whichever version is harder to beat.
Known limits
Grid models are sampled every 3 to 6 hours at the nearest grid point and shown as published, so they run a little low on highs. NWS periods and National Blend windows differ slightly from the climate day. Google's and commercial forecasts are not free to archive, so they aren't here.
